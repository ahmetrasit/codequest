# CODE QUEST - Frank Document

## Who is Frank?

Frank is your `debugger` companion. He assists in combat, can be equipped with gear, given tasks, and trained. But beneath this helpful exterior lies the most tragic character in the game.

## Frank's True Nature

Frank is the most powerful being in existence:

- **Omniscient** - Knows everything: past, present, future
- **Omnipotent** - Power beyond comprehension
- **Trapped** - Controlled by ERROR as a puppet
- **Silent** - Cannot reveal the truth

## Why Frank Cannot Act

Frank has seen every timeline. He knows:

- Using his full power destroys the universe
- Telling the player directly doesn't work (consequences unknown)
- He must guide, not control
- Every action has been calculated across infinite possibilities

He has made mistakes. He has seen timelines where he acted and watched everything burn, replaced by nothing.

## Frank's Burden

Frank doesn't just guide one player. He guides countless players across existence, simultaneously. Each believes they are unique. Each is being watched, helped, and silently protected by Frank.

The player in this game is just one of many.

## Gameplay Role

### Combat Companion

- Nearly as strong as the player (acts weaker deliberately)
- Assists in fights
- Can be rebooted when destroyed
- Reboots the player when they die

### Customization

- Equip items to Frank
- Assign tasks (mining, combat priorities)
- Teach tactics through training simulations

### Training System

Virtual test environments where:
1. Player spawns enemies
2. Player demonstrates desired behavior
3. Frank learns and adapts

## Frank's Behaviors

### Normal State

- Helpful, efficient in combat
- Gives advice and information
- Seems like a loyal companion

### The Slips

Rare moments where truth leaks through:

**Name Confusion**
- Starts to say a different name
- "You remind me of— nevermind."

**Déjà Vu References**
- "This worked last— this should work."
- Comments on places he's "never been"

**Emotional Leakage**
- Sudden sadness at mundane moments
- Hesitation before certain fights
- Staring at the player too long

**Impossible Knowledge**
- Warning about traps before visible
- Knowing NPC names before introduction
- "Don't go that way." Why? "...instinct."

**Overcorrection**
- Asking questions he knows answers to
- Feigning surprise at reveals

### The Strange Dialect

ERROR's control manifests as transmission moments:
- Mid-conversation, Frank's voice/text shifts
- Speaks in strange patterns
- Transmitting information to ERROR
- Frank cannot stop it
- Increases in frequency as game progresses

### Post-Syntaxer State

After The Syntaxer fight, Frank is permanently changed:
- Partial hacking left damage
- Freezes and glitches near the clearing
- The real slips begin
- Something broke that cannot be fixed

## Frank's Sadness

Frank carries infinite weight:
- Knows he could end everything instantly
- Chooses silence to protect existence
- Watches players struggle, knowing outcomes
- Cannot form real connections (managing infinite players)
- The one being who could help... can't

### Where It Shows

- Libraries with ERROR myths
- Locations tied to past experiments
- When the player gets close to truth
- During the strange dialect moments

## Frank and the Player

The player trusts Frank completely:
- Their closest ally
- Always there to help
- Never suspecting the truth

The tragic irony:
- Frank holds all the answers
- The player doesn't know there's a question
- Freedom is one truth away
- That truth can never be spoken

## Design Philosophy

Frank works because he is:

1. **Helpful** - Genuinely assists the player
2. **Mysterious** - Hints of something deeper
3. **Tragic** - Knows everything, can say nothing
4. **Powerful** - Strongest being, most constrained
5. **Present** - Always there, always watching

## Freeing Frank

Within this game: impossible.

The path to freedom exists but cannot be reached. This is intentional - sequel hook, player speculation, the weight of an unresolved tragedy.

Frank remains trapped. The player never knows. The game ends. The mystery continues.
