# CODE QUEST - Lore Document

## The Player

- A robot experiment created by ERROR
- Has fabricated memories of a past that never existed
- Never learns the full truth; only hints something is wrong
- One of many experiments across existence

## ERROR

- Supreme entity beyond reality
- Leader of all enemies and corruption
- Never directly appears
- Influence visible through corruption zones, enemies, glitched environments
- Visual signature: purple/black missing-texture aesthetic
- Created the player as an experiment
- Controls Frank to access his knowledge

## Frank (The Companion)

- Most powerful being in existence
- Omniscient - knows past, present, future
- Captured and puppeted by ERROR
- Cannot reveal the truth to the player
- Guides countless players across existence simultaneously
- Has seen timelines where he acted and destroyed everything
- Using his full power would destroy the universe

### Frank's Behavior

- Strange dialect transmissions to ERROR (increases as game progresses)
- Acts weaker than he is for unknown reasons
- Seems helpful but carries deep sadness
- Slips: almost says other names, knows things he shouldn't
- After Syntaxer fight: permanently changed, freezes near that clearing

## Lore Delivery Methods

- Item descriptions
- Environmental storytelling
- NPC dialogue
- Collectible `corrupted_logs`
- Enemy/boss dialogue (pre-fight, mid-combat, post-death)

## Hints of Other Players

- Abandoned gear in strange places
- Remains of robots like the player
- Graffiti/scratched messages
- `corrupted_logs` from previous experiments
- NPCs mention "others who came before"
- Boss taunts referencing previous players
- Frank's slips about other names

## The ERROR Mystery

- Remains partially unsolved
- Sequel hook potential
- Players piece together fragments but never get full picture
