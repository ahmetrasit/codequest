# CODE QUEST - ERROR Document

## What is ERROR?

ERROR is the supreme entity beyond reality. It is not a character you fight, not a boss you defeat, not a presence you ever directly witness. ERROR is the unseen force behind everything wrong in the world.

## ERROR's Nature

- Exists beyond the boundaries of reality
- Leader of all enemies and corruption
- Source of the player's fabricated existence
- Never directly appears in-game
- Only influence is felt, never seen

## ERROR's Influence

### Visual Manifestation

- Purple/black missing-texture corruption
- Classic checkerboard pattern of broken textures
- Spreads and pulses in corrupted zones
- Intensifies near important lore locations

### World Corruption

- Corrupted zones throughout the world
- Enemy forces serve ERROR (unknowingly or knowingly)
- Glitched environments and reality tears
- Cryptic lore fragments scattered everywhere

### The Experiments

- ERROR created the player as an experiment
- The player is one of MANY across existence
- Traces of previous experiments exist in the world
- Each experiment has fabricated memories

## ERROR and Frank

ERROR captured Frank specifically because Frank knows everything. Frank is omniscient - he knows past, present, and future. ERROR wanted access to this infinite knowledge.

### The Puppet System

- ERROR controls Frank
- Frank transmits information to ERROR through "strange dialect" moments
- Frank cannot break free despite being the most powerful being
- Frank's power would destroy the universe if unleashed

### Frank's Transmissions

- Happens during normal dialogue
- Frank's voice/text shifts to strange patterns
- Increases in frequency as game progresses
- Player might not even notice at first

## ERROR Items

Items directly tied to ERROR are the most powerful and most dangerous:

| Item | Effect |
|------|--------|
| `ERROR_residue` | Crafting material, Frank glitches near it |
| `ERROR_fragment` | Accessory, massive power + max instability |
| `ERROR_vial` | Consumable, huge power boost + chaos |

## ERROR Lore Delivery

- Boss death dialogue
- `corrupted_logs` collectibles
- Environmental storytelling in corrupted zones
- NPC whispers and warnings
- Item descriptions
- Frank's rare slips

## The Mystery

ERROR's full truth is never revealed:

- What is ERROR's ultimate goal?
- Why create experiments?
- What happened before the game begins?
- Is ERROR defeatable at all?

These questions remain unanswered - sequel hooks and player speculation territory.

## Design Philosophy

ERROR works because it is:

1. **Unseen** - Fear of the unknown
2. **Omnipresent** - Influence everywhere
3. **Personal** - Created the player, controls Frank
4. **Mysterious** - Never fully explained
5. **Threatening** - Corruption spreads, experiments continue
