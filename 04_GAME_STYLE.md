# CODE QUEST - Game Style Document

## Visual Style

### Graphics

- Low-poly 3D geometry
- Detailed special effects and shading
- Clean, readable silhouettes
- Corruption effects in specific areas

### Color Palette

- Zone-dependent coloring
- Each area has distinct identity
- Corruption: purple/black (missing texture aesthetic)
- Safe zones: warm, inviting colors
- Dangerous zones: cold, dark tones

### Corruption Visual

- Purple and black checkerboard pattern
- Mimics classic "missing texture" glitch
- Spreads and pulses near ERROR influence
- Intensifies near Frank's dialect slips

## Audio Style

### Music

Hybrid soundtrack combining:
- Action-packed beats
- Emotional orchestral swells
- Ambient atmosphere
- Dark undertones
- Glitch effects (stutters, digital artifacts)

### Dynamic Music System

- Seamless transitions between states
- Layered intensity (base ambient, combat drums, boss swells)
- Zone-specific themes
- Unique boss tracks
- Glitch effects spike near ERROR/Frank slips

### Special Audio

- Faint distant cries in libraries
- Corruption zones have distorted ambiance

### Sound Effects

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Player | Footsteps (surface-based), weapon swings, ability sounds |
| Enemy | Type-specific sounds, communication chatter, captain callouts |
| Environment | Ambient hums, machinery, wind, interactive objects |
| UI | Menu navigation, notifications, cooldown ticks |

### Voice

- Base: Robotic voice
- Bosses: Deeper, menacing variations
- Frank: Distinct voice, distorts during ERROR transmissions
- Dialogue: Grunts and vocalizations, text in speech bubbles

## Camera

### Third-Person (Default)

- Camera behind and above player
- Player controls camera rotation
- Mouse/right stick/touch drag

### Isometric (Settings Option)

- Fixed 45-degree angle
- No camera control
- Simpler for touch devices

## UI Style

- Code-themed interface
- Monospace fonts for stats/numbers
- Terminal-like menus
- Glitch effects on corrupted UI elements
- Clean, minimal HUD during combat
